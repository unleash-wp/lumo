# Social Photos Design Report: /ckm:design Batch 2

## Overview
- **Prompt:** Social photos introducing `/ckm:design` unified design skill (batch 2)
- **Platforms:** Instagram Post (1080×1080), LinkedIn Post (1200×627), X/Twitter Post (1200×675)
- **Variations:** 3 concepts × 3 sizes = 9 images
- **Style:** Dark mode, Ocean Professional (brand-aligned)
- **Design skill:** `/ck:frontend-design` (random pick this run)

## Concepts

### Concept 4: "Design Pipeline"
Visual workflow pipeline — 6 connected steps (Brand → Tokens → Design → HTML → Screenshot → Export) with glowing connection lines. Data-flow aesthetic.
- IG: 3×2 grid with S-curve flow
- LinkedIn/X: horizontal 6-node row with feature pills

### Concept 5: "Swiss Army Knife"
Central hexagonal `/ckm:design` node with 8 capability nodes arranged radially. SVG gradient connection lines.
- IG: centered radial diagram with title above
- LinkedIn/X: 45/55 split — left text + capability grid, right SVG radial diagram

### Concept 6: "Output Showcase"
Cascading fan-spread mockup cards showing tangible outputs (Logo SVG, Business Card, Slide Deck, Banner, Social Post). Realistic card rendering with rotation + shadows.
- IG: centered card fan with title above
- LinkedIn/X: split layout — left text + colored pills, right card fan

## Design Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| Color palette | #3B82F6, #F59E0B, #10B981, #9B5DE5, #FF6B6B, #00F5D4 | Brand Ocean Professional palette |
| Typography | Space Grotesk 700, Inter 400/500, JetBrains Mono | Brand fonts |
| Background | #0D0D0D → #0F1629 → #1A1A2E | Brand dark mode gradient |
| Connection lines | SVG gradients with glow effects | Shows flow/relationship between elements |
| Card mockups | CSS-only realistic renderings | Tangible output demonstration |
| Radial layout | Absolute positioning + SVG | Clean geometric symmetry |

## Why This Works

- **Differentiation from Batch 1:** Completely new visual metaphors (pipeline, radial, showcase) vs previous (grid, typography, terminal)
- **Concept 4 (Pipeline):** Communicates automation + process clarity — shows /ckm:design isn't just output, it's a complete workflow
- **Concept 5 (Swiss Army Knife):** Strongest geometric impact — radial layout immediately communicates "one center, many powers"
- **Concept 6 (Output Showcase):** Most tangible — shows REAL deliverables users get, not abstract features
- **Brand alignment:** 100% Ocean Professional dark-mode identity
- **Text <20%:** Meta ad compliant for paid promotion

## Recommendations

- **Best performer prediction:** Concept 5 (Swiss Army Knife) X post — highest visual impact, clear message, unique layout
- **Instagram carousel:** Use Concept 6 as first slide → individual output deep-dives as subsequent slides
- **LinkedIn:** Concept 4 (Pipeline) resonates with process-oriented professionals
- **A/B test:** Concept 5 vs Concept 6 on X/Twitter — geometric vs tangible appeal
- **Combine with Batch 1:** Total library now 6 concepts × 3 sizes = 18 social images for rotation
