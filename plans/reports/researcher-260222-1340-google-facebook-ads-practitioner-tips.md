## Google Ads — Common Mistakes & Optimization

### Beginner Mistakes
- **Broad Match by default** — Google defaults to broad match; use phrase/exact match until you have data
- **No negative keywords** — Critical omission. Review Search Terms Report every 2 weeks; add negatives aggressively. Google increased limit to 10,000/campaign in 2025
- **Wrong conversion tracking** — Tracking button clicks / page views as conversions destroys optimization signals. Track only real conversions
- **Search + Display Networks on** — Google auto-includes both; disable Display for Search campaigns (wasted spend)
- **No ad variations** — Run 3-5 ad variants per ad group; let data decide winners
- **Impatience** — Smart Bidding needs 2-4 weeks of data; don't change bids weekly

### Optimization Tips
- **Quality Score levers**: landing page relevance > CTR > ad relevance. Match ad copy tightly to keywords
- **Search Terms Report** — Mine it weekly. It's your best source of new keywords AND negative keywords
- **Campaign structure** — Fewer, tightly-themed ad groups > broad ad groups. One theme per ad group
- **Extensions** — Sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets all improve Quality Score and CTR at no cost
- **Smart Bidding** — Use Target CPA or Target ROAS once you have 30+ conversions/month per campaign

## Facebook/Meta Ads — Creative & Scaling

### Creative Strategy
- **Creative = 56% of performance** — Don't skimp on creative quality; it's the #1 lever
- **Creative fatigue is brutal** — Frequency >3.5 + CTR drop >30% = swap creative. Refresh every 10-14 days
- **Test copy first** — Copy variations reveal audience pain points faster than image tests
- **High creative velocity** — Agencies that win run 10-20 active creatives per ad set; chronic under-testing kills scale
- **Hook matters most** — First 3 seconds of video determines everything; test multiple hooks on same body

### Testing Framework
- **ABO for testing → CBO for scaling** — Use Ad Set Budget Optimization to find winners, then Campaign Budget Optimization to scale
- **Isolate variables** — Test one element at a time (image vs. image, headline vs. headline); avoid multi-variable chaos
- **Minimum budget per test** — Don't judge ad performance under $50-100 spend; too little data

### Scaling Without Killing ROAS
- **10-20% budget increases** — Never double budget overnight; algorithm resets learning
- **Duplicate winning ad sets** — Horizontal scaling (new audiences) alongside vertical (budget increase)
- **Advantage+** — Advertisers using Advantage+ features saw 22% higher ROAS; worth testing
- **Retargeting stack** — Warm audiences (visitors, engagers) convert 3-5x better than cold; always run retargeting

## Google Ads vs. Facebook Ads — When to Use Which

| Scenario | Platform |
|----------|----------|
| High purchase intent, active search | Google Ads |
| Demand generation, brand awareness | Facebook/Meta |
| B2B long sales cycle | Facebook (nurture) + Google (BOFU) |
| E-commerce with strong visuals | Both; FB for discovery, Google Shopping for capture |
| Niche interest-based targeting | Facebook |
| Competitive keywords affordable | Google |

**Rule of thumb:** Google captures demand. Facebook creates demand. Best ROI = both, aligned to funnel stage.

## Budget Allocation

- **60/40 split (Google/Facebook)** common starting point for e-commerce; adjust based on data
- **Google:** Set daily budget × 30.4 = monthly ceiling. Google can overspend daily by 2× but not monthly
- **Facebook:** Start small ($20-50/day per ad set), prove CPA, then scale
- **Retargeting always-on** — Even $5-10/day retargeting pays back; don't pause it
- **Reserve 20% for testing** — Earmark budget for creative/audience experimentation every month

## Attribution & Measurement

- **iOS 14.5 broke Facebook attribution** — Reported ROAS is understated; use 7-day click / 1-day view window
- **Google Data-Driven Attribution (DDA)** — Default in 2025; better than last-click for multi-touch journeys
- **Multi-touch reality** — 57% of companies use attribution models; 22% still on last-click (leaving money on table)
- **Server-side tagging** — Use Google Tag Manager server-side to bypass cookie/browser restrictions
- **Triangulate signals** — Platform data + GA4 + revenue CRM + incrementality tests = real picture
- **MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)** — Total revenue / total ad spend; simpler than blended ROAS, more honest
